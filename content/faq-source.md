# Melbourne Commercial Property Maintenance FAQs / 墨尔本商业物业维修常见问题

## 01. Commercial maintenance and service scope / 商业维修与服务范围

### Q01. Do you maintain offices, shops, warehouses and commercial buildings in Melbourne? / 你们维修墨尔本的办公室、商铺、仓库和商业建筑吗？

**EN:** We handle commercial building repairs and maintenance enquiries for offices, retail premises, warehouses, industrial sites and managed properties. We confirm the address, affected area, access conditions and required trades before setting the work scope.

**中文：** 我们承接办公室、商铺、仓库、工业物业及托管商业建筑的维修与维护需求。我们先确认物业地址、故障位置、进场条件和所需工种，再明确施工范围。

### Q02. Can you take care of several repairs during one site visit? / 一个物业有好几处故障，能一次处理吗？

**EN:** We can assess several defects during one visit and group compatible repairs into the same work order. Completion depends on the parts, site access, safety controls and whether licensed trade work is required.

**中文：** 我们可以一次检查多处故障，并将适合同时施工的项目纳入同一工单。能否当次完成，取决于材料配件、现场准入、安全措施以及是否涉及持牌工种。

### Q03. Do you accept small maintenance jobs or only major contracts? / 小型商业维修也接吗，还是只做大型合同？

**EN:** We assess individual repairs as well as recurring maintenance requirements. A damaged door, ceiling panel or leaking fitting can be scoped as a single job; a site with recurring defects can be assessed for scheduled maintenance.

**中文：** 我们既处理单项维修，也评估定期维护需求。门体损坏、吊顶板破损、水管配件漏水等可单独报修；反复出现的故障可以按周期维护安排。

### Q04. Can you maintain several Melbourne sites under one agreement? / 墨尔本有多处门店或物业，能统一报修吗？

**EN:** We can review your repair list and plan jobs by site, priority and access times. Before work starts, we agree on the sites, scope, reporting and available services.

**中文：** 我们可以按物业地址、紧急程度和进场时间整理多站点维修清单。具体覆盖地址、工作范围、汇报格式及到场安排，会在开始前逐项确认。

### Q05. Do you work directly with property managers and facility managers? / 可以直接对接物业经理或设施经理吗？

**EN:** Yes. We can receive work orders through the nominated property or facilities contact, confirm site instructions, report defects and submit completion details against the agreed job reference.

**中文：** 可以。我们按指定的物业或设施管理联系人接收工单，核对现场要求、反馈故障，并按约定工单编号提供完工信息。

### Q06. Can you work while our business remains open? / 商铺或办公室正常营业时能施工吗？

**EN:** We plan work zones around staff, customers and operational areas. Before starting, we confirm noise, dust, access, service shutdowns and whether any section needs temporary isolation.

**中文：** 我们根据员工、顾客及经营区域安排施工分区。开工前确认噪声、粉尘、通行、停水停电及是否需要临时封闭局部区域。

### Q07. Can you follow our building management and tenant access rules? / 能按照大楼管理处和租户的进场规定施工吗？

**EN:** We review the site's induction, contractor approval, loading-bay booking, lift protection and work-permit requirements before scheduling. Where approvals are needed, the authorised building contact must confirm them before work begins.

**中文：** 我们在排期前核对现场培训、承包商准入、卸货区预约、电梯保护和施工许可要求。涉及审批的项目，会在开工前与获授权的大楼管理联系人确认。

## 02. Emergency repairs and response / 紧急报修与响应

### Q08. How soon can you attend an urgent repair at a commercial property in Melbourne? / 墨尔本商业物业紧急维修多久能到？

**EN:** We assess the immediate risk, impact on your business, location and access. Then we let you know the earliest time we can attend.

**中文：** 我们先判断现场风险、营业影响、地址及进场条件，再确认最早可到场时间。

### Q09. Do you take after-hours or weekend emergency jobs? / 下班后、周末或节假日能安排紧急维修吗？

**EN:** We assess out-of-hours requests against the fault, site access and available trade coverage. Before booking, we confirm whether attendance is possible and any applicable after-hours charges.

**中文：** 我们根据故障性质、物业进场条件及工种排班核对非工作时间的维修需求。预约前会明确是否可以到场，以及适用的加急或非工作时间费用。

### Q10. What counts as an emergency commercial maintenance issue? / 哪些商业物业故障属于紧急维修？

**EN:** Active flooding, exposed or sparking electrical equipment, an unsecured entrance, significant storm damage and faults creating an immediate safety risk require urgent assessment. We prioritise making the affected area safe before planning permanent rectification.

**中文：** 持续进水、裸露或打火的电气设备、无法锁闭的入口、严重风暴损伤，以及存在即时安全风险的故障，通常需要优先评估。我们先处理现场安全与止损，再确定永久修复范围。

### Q11. Our shop is leaking during trading hours. Can you make it safe first? / 店铺营业时正在漏水，能先止损吗？

**EN:** We first identify the affected area, water source and electrical exposure. The initial work is to contain the leak and protect the occupied area where it is safe to do so; permanent repairs follow once the cause and access are confirmed.

**中文：** 我们先确认受影响区域、水源和电气风险。具备安全条件时，优先控制漏水和保护营业区域；查明原因并确认进场条件后，再进行永久修复。

### Q12. Can you carry out a temporary make-safe repair before a full repair? / 正式维修前能先做临时安全处理吗？

**EN:** A temporary make-safe may be appropriate when the area cannot be fully repaired immediately. We identify what has been stabilised, what remains unsafe or restricted, and what further work is required.

**中文：** 如果故障无法立即彻底修复，我们会评估可否先进行临时安全处理，并明确已控制的风险、仍需隔离的区域及后续维修内容。

### Q13. What do you need from us when we report an urgent fault? / 紧急报修时需要提供哪些信息？

**EN:** We need the site address, an on-site contact, the affected system, the current risk, access instructions and any immediate trading impact. Photos or a short video help where they can be taken safely.

**中文：** 我们需要物业地址、现场联系人、故障系统、当前风险、进场说明及营业受影响情况。如果可以安全拍摄，现场照片或简短视频有助于判断。

## 03. Quotes, approvals and maintenance records / 报价、审批与维修记录

### Q14. How much does commercial property maintenance cost in Melbourne? / 墨尔本商业物业维修一般怎么收费？

**EN:** The price depends on the fault, labour, materials, site access, safety setup and timing. We distinguish inspection or diagnostic work from the confirmed repair scope so the quote reflects the actual job.

**中文：** 费用取决于故障性质、人工、材料、进场难度、安全措施及施工时间。我们区分现场检查或故障诊断费用与正式维修范围，让报价对应实际工作内容。

### Q15. Is there a call-out or inspection fee? / 上门检查或紧急出工是否收费？

**EN:** We confirm the applicable attendance, inspection or diagnostic charge before the booking. Where the repair cannot be priced without investigation, that work is identified separately from the final repair quotation.

**中文：** 我们会在预约前确认适用的上门、检查或诊断费用。若必须先排查才能确定维修价格，诊断项目会与正式维修报价分开说明。

### Q16. Can you give us a fixed-price quote instead of an hourly estimate? / 可以提供固定总价，而不是只报小时工费吗？

**EN:** A fixed price can be prepared when the defect, materials and access requirements are sufficiently clear. If hidden damage or testing is involved, we first define the investigation and then price the confirmed repair.

**中文：** 当故障、材料和进场要求明确后，可以按确定的施工范围编制固定总价。涉及隐蔽损伤或检测时，我们会先界定排查工作，再对已确认的维修内容报价。

### Q17. What happens if you find additional damage after starting? / 开工后发现额外损坏怎么办？

**EN:** We record the additional defect, explain its impact on the existing scope and provide the proposed variation for approval before carrying out extra chargeable work, except any immediate action needed to prevent danger.

**中文：** 我们会记录新增缺陷，说明对原施工范围的影响，并在产生额外费用的工作开始前提交变更内容供确认；涉及即时安全风险的必要处置会另行说明。

### Q18. Can you work to a purchase order and within our approval limits? / 可以按采购单 PO 和维修审批额度执行吗？

**EN:** We can identify the work-order number, purchase order, site code and approval contact before attendance. We distinguish pre-approved repairs from work that needs a separate variation approval.

**中文：** 我们可以在到场前核对工单号、采购单、站点代码和审批联系人，并区分已授权的维修内容与需要另行批准的变更项。

### Q19. Will we receive photos and a written report after the repair? / 完工后会提供照片和书面维修记录吗？

**EN:** The completion record can identify the fault, work performed, materials or components changed, testing completed and any outstanding items. Before-and-after photographs are included where relevant and permitted by site rules.

**中文：** 完工记录可列明故障、已完成工作、更换材料或部件、检测情况及待跟进事项。适用且现场许可时，可附上维修前后照片。

### Q20. How do you handle insurance, trade licences and safety documents? / 商业项目所需的保险、执照和安全文件怎么核对？

**EN:** We check the documentation required for the specific work and site, including relevant trade registration, licence class, insurance evidence and safety procedures. Regulated electrical, plumbing, gas and refrigerant work must be completed by people authorised for that work.

**中文：** 我们按工种和物业准入要求核对相关执照类别、注册资格、保险证明及安全文件。电气、水管、燃气和制冷剂等受监管作业，必须由具备相应法定资格的人员执行。

### Q21. Can you provide a Safe Work Method Statement (SWMS)? / 施工前能提供 SWMS 安全施工方法说明吗？

**EN:** Where the job involves high-risk construction work, a task-specific SWMS must be prepared before work starts and followed on site. We identify the hazards, controls and responsible persons for the actual work, rather than relying on a generic form.

**中文：** 涉及高风险建筑施工的项目，必须在开工前准备针对该任务的 SWMS，并在现场按文件执行。我们根据实际作业列明风险、控制措施和责任人员，而不是套用通用表格。

## 04. Building fabric, carpentry, doors and partitions / 建筑主体、木作、门窗与隔墙

### Q22. Can you repair damaged walls, plasterboard and suspended ceilings in our office? / 办公室墙面、石膏板和吊顶损坏能修吗？

**EN:** We inspect the damaged lining, framing and any moisture or impact cause before defining the repair. The scope may include patching, board or tile replacement, joint finishing and matching the existing surface.

**中文：** 我们先检查饰面、基层以及受潮或撞击原因，再确定修复范围。工作可包括补板、吊顶板更换、接缝处理及现有表面修复匹配。

### Q23. Our commercial door will not close properly. Can it be repaired? / 商业门关不严、下沉或卡住，能维修吗？

**EN:** We check alignment, hinges, closers, frames, locks and signs of impact damage. We then confirm whether adjustment, hardware replacement or door and frame repair is appropriate.

**中文：** 我们检查门体对位、合页、闭门器、门框、锁具以及撞击损伤，再确定应调整五金、更换配件，还是维修门扇和门框。

### Q24. Can you fix damaged shop counters, shelving and office joinery? / 商铺柜台、货架和办公室定制木作破损能修吗？

**EN:** We assess the joinery structure, fixings, hardware and finish. Depending on the condition, we can scope repairs to cabinet doors, shelving, counters, trims and damaged panels, or quote replacement of individual sections.

**中文：** 我们检查柜体结构、固定件、五金及表面饰面，根据损伤情况确定柜门、货架、柜台、收边和面板的维修范围，或单独报价更换局部构件。

### Q25. Can you repair warehouse wall damage caused by trolleys or forklifts? / 仓库墙面被叉车或推车撞坏能修吗？

**EN:** We assess the impact area and check whether only the lining is damaged or the framing and protection barriers are also affected. Repairs are planned around vehicle movements and the site's exclusion requirements.

**中文：** 我们检查受撞击区域，区分表面板材损坏与内部框架、防撞设施损坏，并按仓库车辆动线和隔离要求安排施工。

### Q26. Can you remove or alter an internal office partition? / 办公室隔墙能拆除或改动吗？

**EN:** We first establish whether the partition affects structure, fire separation, electrical services, HVAC or building approvals. A non-structural alteration is scoped separately from any regulated building or services work.

**中文：** 我们先确认隔墙是否涉及结构、消防分隔、电气管线、空调系统或建筑审批。非承重隔墙调整与受监管的建筑或机电工程会分别明确施工范围。

### Q27. Can you repair commercial windows, frames and weather seals? / 商业窗框、窗户密封条和漏风问题能处理吗？

**EN:** We inspect the frame, operating hardware, glazing seals and surrounding joints. We distinguish a seal or hardware repair from damaged safety glazing or facade work that needs a specialist assessment.

**中文：** 我们检查窗框、开关五金、玻璃密封及周边接缝，区分一般密封或五金维修与需要专项评估的安全玻璃、外立面问题。

## 05. Commercial roofs, box gutters and stormwater / 商业屋顶、箱形天沟与雨水排水

### Q28. Our warehouse roof leaks when it rains. Can you find the cause? / 仓库一下雨就漏水，能找到漏点吗？

**EN:** We inspect roof sheets, laps, fasteners, penetrations, flashings, box gutters and drainage outlets as applicable. We distinguish water entry through the roof from condensation or water backing up in the drainage system before recommending repairs.

**中文：** 我们按现场屋面类型检查屋面板、搭接、固定件、穿屋面节点、泛水板、箱形天沟及排水口，并区分屋面渗入、结露和排水倒灌，再确定维修方式。

### Q29. Can a blocked box gutter cause water to enter our factory? / Box gutter 堵塞会导致厂房内部进水吗？

**EN:** Yes. A blocked or overflowing box gutter can allow stormwater to enter a commercial roof assembly. We check outlets, overflow provisions, gutter condition, roof falls and connected downpipes before defining the repair.

**中文：** 会。Box gutter 堵塞或溢流可能使雨水进入商业屋面结构。我们会检查排水口、溢流设施、天沟状况、屋面坡度及连接的落水管，再确定修复内容。

### Q30. Do we need a full commercial roof replacement, or can the leak be repaired? / 商业屋顶漏水必须整体更换吗，还是局部维修就够？

**EN:** We assess the extent of corrosion, sheet deterioration, flashing faults and drainage problems. Localised defects may justify targeted repairs; widespread failure or repeated leaks may require a staged or full replacement scope.

**中文：** 我们评估锈蚀、屋面板老化、泛水节点和排水问题的范围。局部缺陷可考虑针对性维修；大面积失效或反复漏水，则需要评估分阶段或整体更换。

### Q31. Can you repair storm-damaged COLORBOND® roofing and flashings? / 暴风雨损坏的 Colorbond 屋面和泛水板能修吗？

**EN:** We inspect the affected sheets, fasteners, flashings, roof penetrations and drainage. The repair scope identifies damaged components, access and fall-protection requirements and any temporary weatherproofing needed.

**中文：** 我们检查受损屋面板、固定件、泛水板、穿屋面节点和排水系统。维修范围会注明需更换构件、进场与防坠落要求，以及是否需要临时防雨处理。

### Q32. How often should a commercial roof and gutter system be inspected? / 商业建筑屋顶和天沟应该多久检查一次？

**EN:** Inspection frequency depends on roof design, tree debris, drainage configuration, exposure and previous defects. We set the inspection scope from the asset's condition and applicable maintenance requirements, with additional checks after significant weather events where appropriate.

**中文：** 检查频率取决于屋面构造、落叶杂物、排水布局、暴露环境及历史故障。我们按屋面状况和适用的维护要求确定检查内容，必要时在明显风暴后增加检查。

### Q33. Do commercial roof and stormwater repairs require a licensed trade in Victoria? / 维州商业屋面和雨水排水维修需要对应执照吗？

**EN:** Roofing and stormwater work can fall within Victoria's regulated plumbing classes. We identify the exact task and confirm the relevant registration or licence requirements before arranging roof covering, flashing or drainage alterations.

**中文：** 维州部分屋面及雨水排水工作属于受监管的水管工种类别。我们会按实际作业内容核对相应注册或执照要求，再安排屋面覆盖层、泛水板和排水系统的维修或改动。

## 06. Waterproofing, leaks and moisture damage / 防水、渗漏与潮湿损伤

### Q34. Water is coming through our commercial ceiling. Is it definitely a roof leak? / 商业楼宇天花板滴水，一定是屋顶漏水吗？

**EN:** Not necessarily. We trace the moisture path and check roof drainage, plumbing, air-conditioning condensate and adjacent wet areas as relevant. The visible ceiling stain is not always directly below the entry point.

**中文：** 不一定。我们会沿水迹排查屋面排水、水管、空调冷凝水及相邻湿区。天花板出现水痕的位置，未必就在真实进水点正下方。

### Q35. Can you repair a leaking commercial balcony without removing all the tiles? / 商业阳台漏水，不拆全部瓷砖能修好吗？

**EN:** We first inspect drainage, movement joints, junctions and the waterproofing condition. A localised seal or joint defect may be repairable, but failed membranes or substrate falls can require tile removal and rebuilding of the affected area.

**中文：** 我们先检查排水、伸缩缝、交接节点和防水层状态。局部密封或接缝问题可能可以修补；若防水层失效或基层坡度有问题，则可能需要拆砖并重做受影响区域。

### Q36. Why does the same leak keep coming back after sealing? / 已经打胶补过漏，为什么还会反复渗水？

**EN:** Surface sealing does not correct an underlying membrane failure, failed flashing, trapped moisture or drainage defect. We investigate the source and affected construction layers before specifying another repair.

**中文：** 表面打胶无法解决基层防水层失效、泛水缺陷、内部积水或排水不畅。我们会先确认水源和受影响的构造层，再确定修复范围。

### Q37. Can you find water ingress around shopfronts and external walls? / 商铺门头、橱窗周边和外墙渗水能查吗？

**EN:** We examine window and door junctions, facade penetrations, sealant, flashings and nearby drainage. Testing and opening-up are considered according to the building design and whether the defect can be located without disturbing finishes.

**中文：** 我们检查门窗交接、外墙穿孔、密封胶、泛水节点及附近排水。是否需要专项测试或局部开拆，取决于建筑构造与漏点能否通过非破坏性检查定位。

### Q38. Will you repair the water-damaged ceiling and wall after fixing the leak? / 漏水修好后，受潮天花和墙面也能修复吗？

**EN:** We separate the source repair from reinstatement of damaged linings and finishes. Once moisture conditions and safety have been assessed, the reinstatement scope can include plasterboard, ceiling tiles, patching and painting.

**中文：** 我们将漏水源头修复与受损饰面恢复分开界定。确认含水状况和施工安全后，可安排石膏板、吊顶板、墙面修补和涂装恢复等工作。

## 07. Commercial plumbing, drainage, gas and hot water / 商业水管、排水、燃气与热水

### Q39. Can you fix blocked toilets and overflowing drains at a busy office or restaurant? / 办公室或餐厅厕所堵塞、地漏溢水能处理吗？

**EN:** We assess which fixtures are affected and whether the blockage is local or in a shared drainage line. The work may involve drain clearing, inspection and testing; recurring or deep blockages may need CCTV investigation.

**中文：** 我们先确认受影响洁具和排水点，判断是局部堵塞还是共用排水管问题。作业可包括疏通、检查和测试；反复或深层堵塞可能需要 CCTV 管道检测。

### Q40. Our commercial hot water has stopped working. Can it be repaired? / 商用热水系统突然不出热水，能维修吗？

**EN:** We identify the system type, fault symptoms, water supply and any electrical or gas safety issues. We then assess whether a component repair, further testing or replacement is the appropriate scope.

**中文：** 我们先确认热水系统类型、故障表现、供水情况及相关电气或燃气安全问题，再判断适合更换零件、进一步检测，还是更换设备。

### Q41. We can smell gas in our commercial kitchen. Is that an emergency? / 商用厨房闻到燃气味，算紧急故障吗？

**EN:** Yes. A suspected gas leak is a safety emergency: people should leave the affected area and contact the appropriate emergency service or gas emergency line. Gas testing and repairs must be undertaken by appropriately licensed gas practitioners; the premises must not be treated as safe until assessed.

**中文：** 是。疑似燃气泄漏属于安全紧急事件，应先撤离受影响区域并联系相应紧急服务或燃气应急热线。燃气检测和维修必须由具备相应执照的人员执行，未经检查不能认定现场安全。

### Q42. Why does our warehouse floor drain keep blocking? / 仓库地漏反复堵塞，是什么原因？

**EN:** Repeated blockages can be linked to debris, sediment, grease, roots, damaged pipework or insufficient drainage capacity. We assess the line and use inspection findings to distinguish a cleaning problem from a defect that needs repair.

**中文：** 反复堵塞可能涉及杂物、沉积物、油脂、树根、管道破损或排水能力不足。我们通过管线检查区分单纯清理问题与需要修复的结构性缺陷。
